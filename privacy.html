Privacy policy

Most privacy policies are long because a lot of collecting is going on. This one is short for the opposite reason.

The application

LabFlow LIMS collects nothing. No telemetry, no analytics, no crash reporting, no usage statistics, no account registration. The application blocks its own outbound network traffic by policy, so it could not report on you even by accident.

  • Patient, sample and case data you enter stays in a database folder on your own machine.
  • Backups are encrypted and written only to destinations you choose.
  • Licence activation happens offline — you paste a key; nothing is transmitted.
  • We cannot see your data. Not "we choose not to look" — there is no channel through which it could reach us.
For regulated laboratories this is the whole point: data-residency and patient-confidentiality questions become trivial when the data physically never leaves your building.

This website

  • This site is static pages served by GitHub Pages. We run no analytics scripts, no cookies, no trackers, no embedded third-party widgets.
  • GitHub, as the hosting provider, produces standard server logs (IP address, requested page) under GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive or process those logs.
  • Your language preference (English/Arabic) is stored in your own browser's local storage and never transmitted.

Contact

If you contact us to request a trial or a licence — by email or through our request form — we keep the correspondence and use it to answer you, issue your licence key, and notify you of updates. We do not sell, share or add your address to third-party lists. Ask us to delete the thread and we will.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the change will be visible in this page's history and noted in the changelog. Given the architecture, do not expect much movement.
