Questions · Answered plainly

Everything people ask before trusting us with their lab

Short answers, no hedging. If your question isn't here, ask it on a demo call — the last section covers what LabFlow deliberately doesn't do.

Working offline

Does LabFlow really work with no internet at all?

Yes — completely. The application runs on your PC, stores data on your PC, and actively blocks its own outbound network requests. Even license activation is offline. You could run a lab in a basement with no connection, indefinitely.

Then how do multiple PCs share one database?

Over your own local network (LAN) — no internet involved. One PC hosts the database; others join it. LabFlow finds the host automatically on the network, so there is nothing to configure beyond choosing which machine is the host.

How do I get updates if I'm offline?

Updates ship as a signed installer file. Download it on any connected device, carry it over on a USB stick if you want, and run it. Your database and settings are untouched by updates, and updating is never required — every version keeps working.

Your data

Where exactly is my data stored?

In a database file on your own PC (or your LAN host PC). Backups are encrypted files you place wherever you choose — an external drive, a second PC, a safe. No copy ever exists on our side; we couldn't read your data if we wanted to.

Can I get my data out if I leave?

Yes — CSV export of your records is built in and never locked, even on an expired trial. Your data is yours; holding it hostage would contradict the entire point of the product.

What does the audit trail actually prove?

Every significant action is written to a hash-chained log — each entry is mathematically linked to the previous one. If anyone edits or deletes a past entry, the chain visibly breaks. You can verify the chain from inside the app at any time and show an inspector that records were not silently altered.

Licensing & payment

Is this a subscription?

No. You pay once and the license is perpetual — it never expires. There's an optional yearly updates-and-support plan; if you skip it, LabFlow keeps working forever on the version you have. No lock-in, no ransom.

The license is locked to one device — what if that PC dies?

License reissue for a replacement PC is always free, whether or not you're on the updates plan. Restore your encrypted backup on the new machine, request a reissue, and you're back. Perpetual means perpetual.

How do I pay in Kuwait?

KNET payment link, bank transfer to a local IBAN, or cash on an in-person visit. You receive a signed installer plus your license key by email and WhatsApp — typically same day after payment clears. Every purchase has a 30-day money-back guarantee.

What happens when the 14-day trial ends?

The app switches to read-only: you can view and export everything you entered, but not add new records until a license is activated. Nothing is deleted. If you need longer than 14 days to evaluate seriously, ask about the 90-day pilot instead.

Practical details

What hardware do I need?

Any ordinary Windows 10 or 11 PC from the last several years — 4 GB RAM is enough. No server, no IT department. If your PC runs a browser comfortably, it runs LabFlow.

Is the Arabic real or machine-translated?

Real. Every screen, report, and label is professionally written in Arabic with true right-to-left layout — not a mirrored English interface. You can switch language instantly, and print a report in Arabic for one client and English for the next.

What support do I get, and from whom?

Directly from the developer — WhatsApp and email, in Arabic or English. Licensed labs on the updates plan get priority (same business day). There is no call center and no ticket queue; you talk to the person who wrote the software.

Is LabFlow ISO 17025 certified?

Software cannot be ISO certified — only laboratories can. What LabFlow does is support your accreditation work: tamper-evident records, enforced second-person verification, controlled reports, and complete audit trails map to what assessors ask to see. We say this plainly because vendors who claim "ISO certified software" are misleading you.

What LabFlow doesn't do (yet)

What should I know before buying?

Three honest limits. No live analyzer interfaces — results enter by hand or by CSV import; direct ASTM/HL7 connections are on the roadmap, not in the box. No insurance billing — invoicing is basic; there is no claims submission. No patient portal — offline-by-design means no web access for patients. If any of these is essential to you today, we'd rather tell you now than after you've paid — see the comparison page for who serves those needs better.
