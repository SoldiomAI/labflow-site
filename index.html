FAQ

Questions labs actually ask

Does it really work with no internet at all?

Yes — completely. LabFlow was built for air-gapped environments: it stores everything in a local encrypted-capable database on your PC and even blocks its own outbound network requests. Internet is never required, not even for activation.

Where is my data stored?

In a single database folder on your own machine, with one-click encrypted backups to any drive you choose. You can see the folder from Settings. Nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

Can several people use it at once?

Yes. One PC runs as the server and colleagues connect over your local network with their own accounts and roles — admin, supervisor, analyst, QA, registration. No internet involved.

What happens when my trial ends?

The app locks but your data stays untouched. Activate with a license key and you continue exactly where you left off — nothing is deleted.

What if I replace my computer?

Licenses are locked per device, so you'll get a replacement key for the new machine — restore your encrypted backup and you're running again in minutes.

Is it really bilingual?

Fully. Every screen, report and label works in English and Arabic with true right-to-left layout. Switch anytime from Settings — per user, not per install.
