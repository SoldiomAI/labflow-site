When you should not buy LabFlow

— Your lab depends on live analyser connections (ASTM/HL7). We import CSV exports today; direct interfaces are on the roadmap but not in the box. An enterprise LIMS or a specialised medical SaaS will serve you better now.

— You bill insurance companies daily. LabFlow's invoicing is basic; it does not talk to insurance systems.

— You want a patient portal with online results. LabFlow keeps everything offline by design — that is the point, but it means no patient-facing web portal.

If none of those describe you — and your register is paper or Excel — that is exactly the lab LabFlow was built for.
