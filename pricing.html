The renewal, explained honestly

The optional yearly plan (49 / 89 / 159 KWD by tier) buys you every update we ship — new features, fixes, Windows compatibility — plus priority support and same-day license reissue.

And license reissue after a dead PC is always free, renewal or not. We put this in writing because it's the question every buyer of "one-time" software should ask.
