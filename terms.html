Terms of licence

Written in plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, ask before you buy — you will get an answer from the person who builds the product.

1 · The trial

  • The 14-day trial includes the complete product — every feature, every workspace, both languages.
  • No credit card, registration or internet connection is required.
  • When the trial ends the application locks. Your data is never deleted, and activating a licence resumes exactly where you stopped.

2 · The licence

  • A Professional licence is perpetual and per workstation: pay once, and that machine runs LabFlow indefinitely. Yearly licences are available where budgets prefer operating expenditure.
  • Keys are cryptographically locked to the machine ID you send us. They keep verifying offline — there is no activation server whose fate you depend on.
  • If you replace a machine, contact us with the old and new machine IDs; we issue a replacement key at no charge for genuine hardware moves.
  • Enterprise (multi-seat, LAN) terms are set out in the quote we send you.

3 · Updates and support

  • Professional licences include 12 months of updates from purchase, delivered as installers with checksums and SBOM. After that your software keeps working; extending update access is optional.
  • Priority email support is included for licence holders. Trial users get best-effort answers.

4 · Your data

  • Everything you enter into LabFlow is yours. It lives on your hardware; we never have access to it.
  • There is no exit penalty. Your database remains on your machine and readable by the application for as long as you keep it, licensed or not — a locked application still lets you export your data.

5 · Fair use

  • One licence, one workstation. Do not share licence keys or attempt to bypass licence verification.
  • You may not resell, rebrand or redistribute the software without a written agreement.

6 · Responsibility

  • LabFlow is a records-management tool. Clinical, forensic and regulatory decisions remain the responsibility of the qualified professionals making them.
  • The software is provided as-is. We work hard on correctness — 152 automated tests run on every release — but you are responsible for validating the system in your own environment, keeping backups, and complying with the regulations that apply to your laboratory.
  • Our total liability is limited to the amount you paid for the licence.

7 · Changes

If these terms change, existing licences keep the terms they were purchased under. Changes are noted in the changelog.
