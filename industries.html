Workspaces · Nine industries, one system

Built for the labs that still run on paper

LabFlow adapts its terminology, sample numbering and test catalogue to your field during setup. Below is what that means in practice for each kind of laboratory — including, honestly, where we are not the right fit yet.

Private medical laboratories

Standalone diagnostic labs and small chains, 1–10 staff
  • Patient and sample intake with printed QR labels — no more handwriting sample numbers twice
  • Results entered by the analyst, approved by a second person: four-eyes verification is built in, matching how inspectors expect a lab to work
  • Signed PDF reports in English or Arabic, ready to print or hand to the patient
  • Every action lands in a tamper-evident audit trail — your answer when the Ministry inspection asks "who changed this result?"
  • Basic invoicing per case with payment tracking
Honest scope: LabFlow does not yet connect directly to auto-analysers (results import via CSV) and does not process insurance claims. If your lab lives on analyser interfaces and insurance billing, we are not your system yet — tell us anyway, it shapes the roadmap.

Clinic & medical-centre labs

In-house labs inside polyclinics and medical centres
  • One PC at reception, one in the lab — connected over your own network, no internet involved
  • Roles keep it tidy: registration staff register, analysts enter, the supervisor approves
  • Turnaround-time targets per test, with late work flagged on the dashboard
  • Arabic and English interfaces side by side — each user picks their own

Food, water & environmental labs

Testing labs serving restaurants, farms, municipalities and utilities
  • Chain of custody from sampling point to certificate — who received it, who tested it, who signed it
  • Certificates of Analysis as signed PDFs your clients can trust
  • Reference ranges flag out-of-limit results automatically
  • ISO 17025-minded records: audit trail, calibration log, verification sign-off — evidence your assessor can actually read
Honest scope: LabFlow supports the record-keeping side of accreditation. It is not itself "ISO certified" — accreditation applies to your laboratory, and no software purchase changes that.

Factory & industrial QC

Dairy, bottled water, food processing, plastics, paints, cement
  • Batch and lot testing with pass/fail against your own specifications
  • The QC register your auditor asks for — searchable in seconds instead of a cupboard of binders
  • Certificates of Analysis per batch, printed or PDF, bilingual
  • Equipment calibration schedules with due-date alerts
  • Works on the factory floor PC with no network at all — production networks stay isolated

Pharmacy & pharma QC

Pharmacy QC rooms, small pharma distributors, compounding
  • Incoming-goods checks and release testing recorded per lot
  • Verification sign-off before release — the second signature regulators want to see
  • Reagent and stock levels with low-stock warnings
  • Storage-condition and expiry records in one searchable place

Government & specialist units

Forensic, veterinary, research and municipal units
  • Air-gapped by design: LabFlow blocks its own outbound network requests — data sovereignty isn't a setting, it's the architecture
  • Case-centric workflow with chain of custody and role separation
  • Activation, licensing and updates all work completely offline
  • Arabic-first interface for teams that work in Arabic
